DROP SOURCE VIDEOS HERE
=======================
Save any video you want clipped into this folder, then tell Claude it's here.

Works with: .mp4 .mov .m4v .webm .mkv  (and audio-only .m4a/.mp3 if you just want a transcript)

Why this folder exists: some sources can't be fetched from a link. Read.ai, Zoom cloud, Google
Drive and anything behind a login serve the page but not the media file, so the downloader
can't reach the recording. Downloading it yourself once is the reliable path.
